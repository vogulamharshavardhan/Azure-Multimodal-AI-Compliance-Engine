## General video asset specifications

Google Ads documents 1080p Full HD as the recommended resolution. Common recommended dimensions include 1920 × 1080 pixels for horizontal 16:9, 1080 × 1920 pixels for vertical 9:16, and 1080 × 1080 pixels for square 1:1 assets. The specifications page also lists accepted lower resolutions and aspect ratios. Minimums and accepted formats depend on the placement and campaign type.

The general specification page lists accepted video formats including MPEG-2/MPEG-4 and several other formats. It states a maximum video file size of 256 GB for the described video assets. Audio-only files are not accepted as video assets.

## Campaign-specific length and creative requirements

Video length requirements and recommendations vary by campaign and ad format. For example, the official video formats page lists different recommendations for in-feed, in-stream, bumper, Shorts, and Masthead placements. Do not apply a single duration limit to every YouTube ad. Evaluate a length requirement only after the exact campaign/ad format is established from reliable context. If the format is unknown, mark applicability or the requirement as uncertain and request human review.

Text assets, thumbnails, companion banners, safe zones, and other creative requirements are also format-specific. Retrieve the exact current official specification for the identified campaign type rather than inferring a universal rule from this summary.

## Applicability boundary

An ordinary organic YouTube upload is not automatically a paid advertisement and must not be evaluated against paid-ad creative specifications unless the paid-ad placement is established. The pipeline must preserve the requester-provided placement context and avoid inferring paid-ad status solely from the fact that a video is hosted on YouTube.
